SAFETY GUIDE

Know what to do

Simple general guidance for common emergency situations.

01
🚨

In an emergency

Move to a safe location if possible, stay calm and contact the appropriate emergency service.

02
🔥

Fire safety

Leave the affected area safely, avoid elevators and contact fire and rescue services.

03
🚗

Road accident

Stay away from immediate traffic danger, contact emergency services and avoid unnecessary movement of injured people.

04
🌧️

Natural disasters

Follow official instructions, move to a safer location and monitor trusted alerts.

05
📱

Stay connected

Keep your phone charged and share important information with trusted people when appropriate.

06
🧠

Stay calm

Clear thinking can help you make safer decisions and communicate useful information to emergency responders.

ABOUT LIFELINK

Built around one simple idea: help should be easier to find.

LIFELINK is a student-built safety and emergency-resource platform designed to bring useful information together in one simple interface.

The project is being developed step by step, with reliability and accessibility as core goals.

✓
Simple

Designed to be understandable when users need information quickly.

✓
Accessible

Responsive design for phones, tablets and computers.

✓
Information focused

Built around useful public safety information rather than unnecessary complexity.
